NovaCalc – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

NovaCalc ("the app") is a calculator, unit converter and finance tool for Android, published by Lalith Madanayake ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

NovaCalc does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It works entirely on your device, needs no internet connection, requests no permissions, and shows no ads.

Data stored on your device

To work properly the app saves the following only in the app's private storage on your phone: your settings (theme, number grouping, angle unit, vibration, language) and your recent calculation history (up to 100 entries). This data never leaves your device through the app. You can clear the history from the History screen, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If you have Android backup enabled, Android may include this small amount of app data in your own Google backup. That is handled by your device and Google under your own account settings, not by me.

Permissions, ads and third parties

  • The app requests no Android permissions (no contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or internet).
  • There are no advertisements, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries in the app.
  • No data is shared with any third party, because none is collected.

Children

NovaCalc is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Calculation accuracy

NovaCalc is provided for convenience. Results of finance tools (loan EMI, tax, discounts) are estimates; check important figures with your bank or accountant.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before the change is released.
